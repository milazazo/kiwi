welcome to the aotearoa new zealand webring!

Kiwebring is a weblisting for non-commercial sites and blogs based in Aotearoa (New Zealand). It connects websites from NZ via a cute little widget you place on your site. For more information on webrings, visit indieweb.org.

rules

  • You can be currently or previously based in New Zealand (if previously based here, please specify in the form). Your content doesn't need to be NZ-focused.
  • This isn't limited to personal websites; portfolio sites and non-commercial sites run by groups are also welcome as long as you follow the other rules.
  • No single page websites (Carrd, Strawpage, etc.), social media (Tumblr, Instagram, etc.), or commercial websites.
  • No websites with hateful (racist, homophobic, transphobic, etc.) content.
  • You must link back to the webring with one of the codes within 1 month of being added as a member.
  • An 88x31px button is preferred, but if you don't have one I'll use a placeholder with your site name.
